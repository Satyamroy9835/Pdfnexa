Privacy & Data Safety

Your documents remain 100% your private property. At PDFNexa, privacy is our baseline standard:

  • Zero Server Storage: Conversions operate locally inside your browser sandbox whenever possible.
  • No Content Mining: Document contents, images, and files are never cataloged, processed, or shared.
  • Encrypted Connections: Complete SSL/TLS transport layer security across all sessions.
